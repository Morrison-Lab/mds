The algebra notes are split across these pages:

- The [Equalities, Inequalities and Minimizers](algebra-basics.llms.md) page: equalities, inequalities, powers, square roots and absolute values, minima and maxima, and global and local minimizers.
- The [Convexity, Infimum and Supremum](algebra-convexity.llms.md) page: [convex functions](algebra-convexity.llms.md#def-convex-function), and the [infimum](algebra-convexity.llms.md#def-infimum) and [supremum](algebra-convexity.llms.md#def-supremum).
- The [Sums, Products and Quotients](algebra-sums.llms.md) page: sums, products and quotients, summation notation, limits of sums, and notational shorthands.
- The [Polynomials, Affine Functions and Series](algebra-polynomials.llms.md) page: polynomials, affine and linear functions of one variable, limits of [sequences](https://morrison-lab.github.io/mcs/functions.html#def-sequence), and [series](algebra-polynomials.llms.md#def-infinite-sum).
- The [Exponentials and Logarithms](algebra-exponentials.llms.md) page: exponentials and logarithms.
- The [Trigonometry](algebra-trigonometry.llms.md) page: cosine and sine on the unit circle, and the identities later pages use.
- The [Complex Numbers](algebra-complex.llms.md) page: complex numbers and their arithmetic.

A link to a section, theorem or definition on this page from before the split goes on to the page that now holds it.

# 1 Further reading

- Abramson et al. [\[1\]](#ref-abramson2021algebra) is a free online textbook on algebra and trigonometry. It covers equations, inequalities, polynomials, exponentials, logarithms, and trigonometry, which overlap with the algebra in these notes.
- Rudin [\[2\]](#ref-rudin1976principles) develops the real numbers, including the least upper bound property behind the infimum and supremum.
